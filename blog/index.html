Frequently Asked Questions About Hardwood Floor Installation and Refinishing

How much does hardwood floor installation and refinishing cost in Spokane?

For a typical Spokane home of 1,000 to 1,500 square feet, refinishing generally runs $4 to $7 per square foot. New installation varies widely with the wood type and the complexity of the job. The condition of the subfloor, the finish you choose and any repairs all affect the final quote. Estimates are free and come with no obligation.

How long does hardwood floor installation and refinishing take?

For a typical single-family home of around 1,000 to 1,500 square feet, a complete refinishing project usually takes 3 to 5 days. New installation can take 5 to 7 days, depending on acclimation time and complexity.

What impact does Spokane's climate have on hardwood floors?

Spokane has dry, hot summers and cold, damp winters. Those swings make wood expand and contract, which can cause gaps or warping over time. Proper acclimation before installation and a durable, moisture-resistant finish help floors hold up year-round.
